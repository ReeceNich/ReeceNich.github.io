Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not use any tracking or analytics cookies.

The only data saved on your device is in your browser's local storage (theme) when you toggle between light and dark mode. This remembers your preferred theme. It stays on your device and is never sent to me or any third party.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have rights to your personal information, including the right to ask for a copy of any personal data I hold about you or to ask for it to be deleted.

Since I don't have any sign ups or track individual visitors, I don't hold personal browsing data. However, for any queries, you can reach out to me at contact@reecenicholls.co.uk.

If you have unresolved queries about how your data is handled, you have the right to contact the UK data protection supervisory authority:
